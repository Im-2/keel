Problem

Bitcoin holders want dollars
without selling, and without babysitting a loan.

01

Selling hurts

Selling BTC to pay for life gives up the upside and can trigger tax. Borrowing is better, but on-chain loans live in dashboards most people never open.

02

Loans need watching

A price drop at 3 a.m. can liquidate a loan below 110%. On MUSD, low-ratio loans are also redeemed first. Most borrowers find out too late.

03

Dollars are hard to use

Paying a friend, splitting dinner or paying someone without a wallet is still clumsy on-chain, so borrowed dollars sit idle.

Solution

One account, four pillars.

01

Borrow, with guardrails

“borrow 1800 at 160%” in chat or the web app. Fee, fixed interest, ratio and liquidation price before you sign; loans Mezo would reject are blocked.

02

Guardian protection

Each loan lives in the owner's guardian contract. Below the trigger, a permissionless keeper tops it up from the owner's reserve. Nothing else.

03

Risk alerts

Liquidation distance, redemption-queue rank and Recovery Mode, each with a one-tap fix ready to sign.

04

Payments

Send to any @username, split bills, and send escrowed claim links to people with no wallet. Keel pays their gas.

Non-custodial: the server only creates intents; the user signs everything in their own wallet. In Telegram as a Mini App, or in any browser with wallet sign-in.

6
Guardian protection

Protection that acts, not just alerts.

  • Owner funds a reserve and picks a trigger and target.
  • Anyone may call protect() below the trigger; it can only move reserve into the owner's own loan. Tip ≤ 1%, cooldown, owner-only withdrawals.
  • v2 partial rescue: too small a reserve still helps as far as Mezo allows, and reports exactly how much BTC to add. Below 110% in Normal Mode, Mezo rejects any top-up that doesn't clear 110%: v2 returns “insufficient” with the shortfall instead of reverting.
  • Proven on testnet: {{PROT_TOTAL}} protections, {{PROT_PRICE}} triggered by real BTC price moves.

Payments

Pay anyone. Even without a wallet.

Send to @username

From chat or the app. Shown as sent only once confirmed on Mezo; the recipient gets a Telegram notice.

Split bills

Equal, custom or percentage shares on a live card in a group. Each payment is matched on-chain.

Claim links

MUSD escrowed in KeelPay; share a link; the recipient claims in one step and Keel pays the gas. Refunds after 72h if unclaimed.
