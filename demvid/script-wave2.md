# Keel — Wave 2 demo video script

Voice: ElevenLabs "Matilda – Knowledgable, Professional" (premade, `XrExE9yKIg1WjnnlVkGX`, found via `GET /v1/voices`),
`eleven_multilingual_v2`, stability 0.5, similarity 0.75, one generation per section. Every line is backed by a shot in
`footage-log-wave2.md`. Numbers are written out so the voice reads them correctly.

What is said is what is true today: the three Guardian v2 rescue paths were run on Mezo **testnet** with the team's own
fresh test wallet; the protocol-fee events are real but tiny testnet amounts; no outside user has used Keel yet.

## 1 · Hook  (motion graphics)
A Bitcoin-backed loan can be liquidated while you sleep. Spending dollars usually means selling your Bitcoin. Keel: dollars against your Bitcoin, protected automatically.

## 2 · What Keel is  (four pillars, motion graphics)
Keel is a Bitcoin-backed dollar account on Mezo. Borrow MUSD without selling. A Guardian contract protects the loan. Alerts warn you early. Payments reach anyone, even without a wallet.

## 3 · Telegram  (Wave 1 phone footage: quote card, protection, ratio 160% to 180%)
In Telegram, you ask for a loan in plain English, review the fee and liquidation price, and sign in your own wallet. When the ratio slips, a keeper tops it up from your reserve.

## 4 · New: web and docs  (landing page, web app sign-in, demo mode, docs, deck)
New since Wave One: Keel opens in any browser, no Telegram needed. Sign in with a wallet, or try the read-only demo. The new site has fifteen pages of documentation, and a pitch deck.

## 5 · Guardian v2  (three outcomes graphic, explorer transactions, verified source)
Guardian version two never fails just because the reserve is small. It completes the rescue, or rescues as far as the reserve allows, or, if Mezo would reject any top-up, does nothing and tells you how much Bitcoin to add. We ran all three on Mezo testnet with a fresh wallet. Every contract is verified on the explorer.

## 6 · Stats and revenue  (stats page, protocol fees, revenue chips)
The stats page reads the chain: guardians, loans, and every protection with its transaction. With our own test wallets included, three guardians; outside users are still to come. The first protocol fees are on-chain, tiny testnet amounts. Revenue starts with a capped share of keeper tips, live today, then Keel Pro and merchant checkout.

## 7 · Payments  (Wave 1 phone footage: send, split, claim link)
Send to a username, split a bill, or share a claim link a friend can claim without a wallet.

## 8 · Close  (architecture slide, then end card)
Everything settles in MUSD on Mezo, and Keel never holds your keys.
