# Keel — demo video script (Mezo Buildathon)

Voice: ElevenLabs "Matilda", multilingual v2, stability 0.5, similarity 0.75. Every line below is backed by a shot in
`footage-log.md`; nothing is narrated that the recording doesn't show.

## 1 · Hook  (motion graphics)
Bitcoin holders face a bad trade-off. Sell your Bitcoin to spend dollars, or borrow against it and live one price drop away from liquidation.

## 2 · What Keel is  (logo reveal + architecture explainer)
Keel fixes that, inside Telegram. You chat with a bot, review in a Mini App, and sign in your own wallet. Your loan is held by a personal Guardian contract on Mezo, and a keeper can top it up from your reserve before trouble starts.

## 3 · Borrow  (footage 0:04–1:20)
Connect any wallet with a single signature. Then just say what you want: borrow eighteen hundred MUSD at a hundred and sixty percent. Keel replies with the full quote: collateral, fees, interest and your liquidation price. Nothing moves until you review and sign. Two signatures later, your Guardian opens the loan, and the MUSD is in your wallet.

## 4 · Protection  (footage 1:40–2:29)
Next, put a little Bitcoin in your reserve and turn on protection, again in plain English. For this demo, the trigger sits above the current ratio. Within a minute, a keeper calls the Guardian, which adds Bitcoin from the reserve. The ratio climbs from one-sixty to one-eighty percent, and the liquidation price drops by about six thousand dollars.

## 5 · Payments  (footage 2:31–3:17, 3:18–3:57, 4:03–4:12; two phones)
Moving dollars is just as easy. Send MUSD to a friend by username, and they see it arrive on their phone. Split a bill and Keel tracks who still owes. Or send a claim link: your friend opens it, claims in one tap, and Keel pays the gas.

## 6 · Close  (motion graphics + end card)
Everything settles on Mezo, in Bitcoin-backed MUSD, and Keel never holds your keys or your funds. Keel: dollars against your Bitcoin, right inside Telegram.
